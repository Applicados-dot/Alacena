Alacena Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · App version: 1.0.0

Alacena is an application for managing household inventory, tracking expiration dates, and creating shopping lists. It has been designed with privacy by default in mind: it works entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or share personal data.

1. Data Controller

Developer: Applicados
Contact: infogooweb@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • The application does not require user registration.
  • We do not request or store names, email addresses, or passwords.
  • Products, batches, locations, shopping lists, and settings are stored only on the device.
  • Backups created by the application are generated and controlled by the user.
  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics.

3. Local Storage

All Alacena data is stored in a SQLite database located in the application's private storage area on the device.

  • Data is never sent to external servers.
  • Data is not synchronized with cloud services.
  • All information remains under the user's control.

4. Camera Permission

The application may request access to the device camera in order to scan product barcodes.

  • The camera is used exclusively to read barcodes.
  • Captured images are processed locally on the device.
  • No images or videos are transmitted to external servers.
  • No photographs are stored by the application.

5. Notification Permission

Alacena may display local notifications related to products approaching their expiration date or reminders configured by the user.

  • Notifications are generated entirely on the device.
  • No external notification services are used.
  • Notifications can be disabled through the application settings or the device settings.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect contacts, photographs, or personal documents.
  • We do not use tracking or telemetry tools.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • We do not sell user information.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Alacena does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising networks.

The application does not use analytics, user tracking, behavioral profiling, or remote usage monitoring services.

8. Data Export and Backups

Alacena allows users to export data and create backups.

  • Exported files are stored only in the location chosen by the user.
  • The protection of exported files outside the application is the user's responsibility.
  • The application does not retain external copies of exported files.

9. Children

Alacena is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

10. Security

The application has been designed to minimize data exposure by using local-only storage. However, no system can guarantee absolute security, and users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and any backup files they create.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect improvements to the application or legal requirements. The most recent version will replace all previous versions.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the operation of Alacena, you may contact the developer at:
